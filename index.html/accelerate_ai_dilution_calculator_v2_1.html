These are materially different — a $5M cap gives investors ~20% of the company on $1M; a $7M cap gives ~14.3%. You must reconcile before any investor executes. Issuing SAFEs at different caps to different investors creates §5(a) amendment complexity.
⚠
Discount Rate Notation: Your SAFE document states the Discount Rate is 80% (not 20%). Per §2, Discount Price = Series A lowest price × Discount Rate (80%). This means investors pay 80 cents per dollar of Series A price — equivalent to a 20% discount. This is standard YC language but counterintuitive. The calculator applies the correct 80% multiplier.
✓
Option Pool Structure: §2 definition of "Company Capitalization" explicitly includes the Unissued Option Pool. This means creating your option pool pre-SAFE is legally clean — it increases the denominator for Cap Price calculation, reducing shares issued to SAFE investors. No SAFE amendment required.
✓
Virginia Law Governing: §5(f) — this SAFE is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Virginia. Your company is incorporated as a Virginia corporation, consistent with the governing law clause. No jurisdiction conflict.
✓
Tax Treatment: §5(g) — the parties agree to treat this SAFE as Common Stock for US federal and state income tax purposes (§§304, 305, 306, 354, 368, 1036, 1202 IRC). This is the standard YC position and supports potential QSBS (Qualified Small Business Stock) treatment for investors, which is a selling point.
// Key Defined Terms — SAFE §2
Cap Price
Post-Money Valuation Cap ÷ Company Capitalization (calculated immediately prior to Equity Financing). This is the maximum effective price per share the SAFE investor pays. Lower cap = more shares = more dilution to you.
Source: SAFE §2 "Cap Price"
Company Capitalization
Includes: all issued/outstanding Capital Stock + Converting Securities (other SAFEs, convertible notes) + issued/outstanding Options + Promised Options + Unissued Option Pool (with exception for increases at Equity Financing). This is the denominator in Cap Price — expanding it with a pre-SAFE option pool raises Cap Price and reduces SAFE investor dilution to you.
Source: SAFE §2 "Company Capitalization"
Discount Price
Lowest price per share of Standard Preferred Stock in the Equity Financing × 80% (Discount Rate). The 20% discount applies to the lowest Series A price — if Series A has multiple share classes, SAFE investors get the best deal.
Source: SAFE §2 "Discount Price"
Liquidity Event
Means a Change of Control, Direct Listing, or IPO. Upon a Liquidity Event before conversion, §1(b) entitles the investor to the greater of (i) the Purchase Amount (cash-out) or (ii) as-converted proceeds at Liquidity Price. This is the investor's downside protection — they cannot lose principal in a sale unless proceeds are insufficient for all pari passu holders.
Source: SAFE §1(b), §2 "Liquidity Event"
Amendment / Majority-in-Interest
§5(a) allows amendment by the Company + majority-in-interest of SAFEs with the same Cap/Discount. "Majority-in-interest" = holders with >50% of total Purchase Amount of that group. If you issue multiple SAFEs at $5M cap / 20% discount, you need holders representing >$500K+ to amend terms — a governance risk if you have a single large investor. Recommendation: consider separate cap/discount tiers if negotiating with different investors.
Source: SAFE §5(a)
